# The Chiron Dictionary — the vault's vocabulary

Every term this project uses with a precise, load-bearing meaning. Companion
to the [ENCYCLOPEDIA](ENCYCLOPEDIA.md) (per-module reference, generated from
the manifest) and the [SOP](SOP.md) (how to operate everything). Terms in
*italics* are themselves defined here.

---

**abstain / refuse.** The engine's answer when it cannot exactly prove a rule:
it declines to certify rather than guess. Refusal is the product, not a
failure mode — the whole vault exists so that a *stamp* means something.

**articulate.** The inverse of *collapse*: speak a recovered *invariant* back
as terms, text, or explanation. `chiron.articulate(inv)`.

**attestation.** The tamper-evident SHA-256 hash on a *certificate*. It proves
the certificate wasn't altered after emission; it is explicitly **not** a
cryptographic signature (no key, no identity claim).

**battery** (gate battery). The full set of test suites run before any claim
of "works": engine stress gates, internal benchmark, certify gates, fuzz,
MCP handshake, certify property grid, live/cached OEIS, drift, parity,
monolith sweep. `python3 bin/chiron test` runs the standard battery;
see the [SOP](SOP.md#the-gate-battery) for the full table.

**beat.** One cycle of the *heartbeat*: inward (read an organ into the heart
*Congress*), outward (a grower pass; dry-run unless `CHIRON_HEART_LIVE=1`),
reflex (rotate real battery gates), witness (emit the *vault certificate*).
A beat is GREEN only if no movement failed; the certificate never flatters.

**Candor.** The anti-patronization engine (`Candor/`): audits language for
softening, hedging, and flattery. Inside Chiron it is the `audit` verb — the
Wisdom layer.

**certificate.** The auditable JSON a *certifying* run leaves behind
(`Chiron/artifacts/<module>/latest.json`): what was claimed, what was proven,
`self_hash`, and the two human-view lines that matter — *what was discovered*
and *what would falsify this*.

**certify.** The Primus front door for LLM/agent output: split a text into
checkable claims, judge each **VERIFIED / REFUTED / REFUSED** by exact
methods, report the *unverifiable remainder* honestly, emit *coverage* and an
*attestation*. `primus certify - --gate` exits 1 on any refuted claim. The
contract is written in `Primus/SCHEMA.md`.

**Chiron.** The flagship engine and organism (`Chiron/chiron.py` plus ~70
modules): recover exactly, verify, refuse, certify — wrapped in memory
(*Congress*), a *heartbeat*, servers, and a dashboard. Named for the centaur
who taught heroes: knowledge with a duty of care.

**collapse.** The core verb: take an ambiguous *surface* and recover the
minimal *generator* beneath it under *MDL*, in exact arithmetic, verified by
*holdout*. `primus collapse "1 1 2 3 5 8"`.

**Congress.** Chiron's grown memory (`chiron_memory.json`): the reusable laws
it has earned, newest last — a permanent, replayable research journal. The
heart keeps its own smaller congress, self-grown from the vault's organs.

**coverage.** The fraction of an input that certify could even check
(claims it recognized vs. free text). Reported on every certificate so
"nothing refuted" can never quietly mean "nothing checked."

**dictionary / encyclopedia / SOP.** This trio: vocabulary (hand-written),
per-module reference (generated from the *manifest*), operating manual
(hand-written). The encyclopedia regenerates; the other two are curated.

**drift.** The two-engine risk: the *seed* and *Chiron* implement one idea
and can silently diverge. `Primus/drift_check.py` runs both engines over a
fixed surface set (42 surfaces incl. every historical false stamp) and fails
on any contradiction. A deliberate seed lead is recorded in the
**SEED_AHEAD_LEDGER** with a dated reason; unledgered divergence fails CI.

**evidence rule (h ≥ p).** A model may stamp only if the held-out evidence
scales with its capacity: an order-p *linear recurrence* (2p parameters)
needs at least p held-out hits. Adopted 2026-07-11 after A002808 (composites)
fit 12 terms exactly and was judged by only 3. See *holdout*.

**exact arithmetic.** No floats on any stamping path: Fractions, exact
integer equality, floats ≥ 2⁵³ refused outright. Every false verification
this project has ever shipped traced back to a float where a Fraction
belonged (repunits, companion Pell, A001147).

**falsifier.** The one concrete observation that would break a claim, stated
on every certificate. If a claim has no falsifier, it isn't a claim.

**fold.** The single-file build of the whole spine:
`Chiron Monolith/chiron_monolith.py`, ~68 modules embedded byte-identical,
regenerated by `build_monolith.py`, proven by `--selftest` (same coverage as
the full build). A *lossless fold, not a rewrite* — and never hand-edited.

**gate.** One boolean check with a name. Gates aggregate into *selftests*,
selftests into the *battery*. A failing gate is information, not an obstacle
— the project's best finds were gates doing their job.

**generator.** The minimal rule that reproduces a *surface*: a recurrence,
polynomial, ratio law, period, grammar. What *collapse* recovers and
*holdout* proves.

**Governance.** The layer (`Governance/`, `Chiron/govern.py`) that clears a
verified finding before it becomes a recommendation — bounded agency on top
of exact knowledge.

**grower.** The machinery that extends the *Congress* from new material
(`chiron_grow.py`, `president_grow.py`, feeds in `grow-public/`): the model
proposes, the engine verifies, only verified laws land.

**heartbeat.** `Chiron/heartbeat.py` — the vault moving on its own pulse,
unattended: see *beat*. Runs under `chiron serve`; harness off the tempo,
never the truth.

**holdout.** The proof step: refit on a prefix, predict the held-out tail
(h = max(2, n∕4) terms), demand exact equality. Since 2026-07-11 the refit
must also reproduce its **own prefix** exactly (*prefix-exactness*) and obey
the *evidence rule* — a rule that misfits what it was fitted on has recovered
nothing, and tail hits alone can be luck.

**incarnations.** The two runnable forms of Chiron: the spine
(`Chiron/*.py`) and the *fold*. *Parity* proves they behave identically.

**incompressible.** The honest negative: no model in the hypothesis class
compresses the surface. Returned as a classification, never dressed up as a
finding.

**Infectatrum.** Side engine (`Infectatrum/`): a universal
ambiguity/coherence engine grounded in Juan Caramuel's *Primus Calamus* — the
vault's digital-humanities root.

**invariant.** The engine's result object: domain, model class, parameters,
bits, residuals, `verified`, explanation, predictor. The thing a certificate
memorializes.

**JDICert.** Side engine (`JDICert/cert_engine.py`): a monolithic
certification engine for high-stakes autonomous decisions — an ancestor of
the certify discipline.

**lexicon.** `Chiron/lexicon.json`: per-module title plus three lenses
(mathematically / programmatically / conceptually). The human vocabulary the
dashboard and *encyclopedia* draw from.

**manifest.** `Chiron/manifest.json`, built by `build_manifest.py`: the
machine-readable truth about every runnable script — purpose, gates, deps,
the internal import graph (*imports / imported_by*), roles, last run, last
certificate. Since 2026-07-10 it is a build graph, not a list.

**MDL** (minimum description length). The selection criterion: the best
generator is the one that shortens the total description (model bits +
residual bits) the most — Occam, made arithmetic.

**monolith.** See *fold*. Also the folder `Chiron Monolith/` that carries it
plus the bundled dashboard and data for standalone runs.

**parity.** The gate suite run through BOTH *incarnations* with identical
outcomes required — 138 named gates. PARITY GREEN is the proof that the fold
is the spine.

**playground.** The browser demo
(`https://jiannotti5040.github.io/chiron-vault/playground.html`): the real
engine sources on CPython/WebAssembly, fetched verbatim from the repo, running
client-side. Not a mock.

**plugins.** Drop-in modules next to the *monolith* (`plugins/<name>.py`).
Two enforced rules: **embedded always wins** (a plugin can add, never shadow
a certified module) and plugins sit outside the fold's claims (`--selftest`
sweeps embedded only). Prototype.

**prefix-exactness.** See *holdout*. Adopted 2026-07-11 (A000002 Kolakoski);
it also exposed Thue-Morse's cached stamp as the same luck.

**President.** The bounded-agency contract: the model **proposes**, the
engine **disposes**, anything irreversible **escalates** to a human. Lifted
from single actions to composed campaigns by the *planner* (HORIZON H2.1,
prototype).

**Primus.** The seed: the installable package (`pip install ./Primus`,
dist name `primus-intelligence`) carrying the exact engine
(`src/primus/engine.py`), *certify*, the CLI, and the MCP server
(`primus-mcp`). One implementation per idea — the seed and *Chiron* are held
together by *drift* and *parity*.

**Quack System Constructs.** Side collection (`Quack System Constructs/`):
conceptual system constructs; see its README.

**run ledger.** `Chiron/run_ledger.py`: append-only, crash-healing witness of
every invocation (CLI, console, assistant, heart). What the dashboard's Pulse
streams.

**SCHEMA / schema string.** `Primus/SCHEMA.md` + `primus.certificate/2`: the
written contract certify's consumers gate on. Field changes bump the string.

**seed / flagship / spine.** The seed = *Primus* engine (packaged, minimal).
The flagship/spine = *Chiron* (the full organism). Two incarnations of one
discipline; see *drift* and *parity*.

**selftest.** A module's own gate suite, runnable as
`python3 <module>.py selftest` (or through the fold). 52 modules carry one;
the manifest records every result.

**semic.** The semantic calculus (`Chiron/semic.py`, 56 gates): the same
collapse/verify/refuse contract applied to meaning rather than integers.

**stamp.** To mark VERIFIED. The one act this project treats as sacred:
*zero false verifications* is the invariant, refusal the default, and every
stamp carries a *falsifier*.

**surface.** Any input with structure: a number sequence, a cipher, code, a
grid, text with figures in it. What *collapse* consumes.

**twins / twin cross-lock.** The paired exact engines (seed + Chiron) and the
gate (`test_twins_exact.py`) that locks their behavior together on shared
surfaces.

**UMA Suite.** Side project bundle (`UMA Suite/`): the UMA/RSLS deliverable,
provenance-chained (`MANIFEST.sha256` — chain-hashed; do not remove archive
members, it breaks the chain).

**unverifiable remainder.** The part of a certified text that exact methods
cannot check. Reported, never blessed — see *coverage*.

**vault.** This repository: the engines plus everything that keeps them
honest (battery, drift, parity, ledgers, certificates, records). Grows
outward (users, external validation, exactness), not inward.

**vault certificate.** The heartbeat's one signed self-statement
(`Chiron/artifacts/vault/latest.json`): beat, incarnation, fold hash, both
memories, movements, falsifier. Any failed movement ⇒ not green.

**verified.** Exact equality on data the rule never saw — never a tolerance,
never a vibe. See *holdout*, *stamp*, *zero false verifications*.

**Veritas.** The knowledge-and-wisdom engine (`Veritas/`); Chiron's error
type (`VeritasError`) carries the name.

**zero false verifications.** The one inviolable property: the engine must
never stamp what is wrong. Falsified three times by external data (repunits
2026-07-04, companion Pell 2026-07-07, the three-miss sweep 2026-07-11) —
each time published immediately and repaired at root, which is exactly the
system working. The claim is stronger for its scars.
